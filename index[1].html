the problem

You've never met the person who grew your vegetables.

Most produce passes through three or four hands before it reaches your kitchen — each one adding cost, days, and distance from the source. The farmer gets a fraction of what you pay, and you get no idea where any of it came from.

Know the farmer

Every listing carries a real profile — the farm's location, growing practices, and reviews from other households.

Trace the source

Track produce from the field it was picked in to the doorstep it arrives at, with nothing hidden along the way.

Support your locality

Distribution runs through trusted households in your own neighborhood, keeping value close to home.

how it moves

From the field to your door, in three steps.

Produce leaves the farm the same day it's picked, arrives at a local household acting as a distribution point, and reaches you through a delivery partner or self-pickup.

01 · farm

Picked fresh

Vegetables are collected directly from verified farms and brought into the city the same day.

02 · locality

Produce first arrives at a trusted household in your area, acting as a neighborhood distribution point.

03 · home

To your door

A local delivery partner brings it to you — or you can collect it yourself from the household nearby.

why it matters

The more you buy, the stronger the relationship gets.

This isn't a one-time transaction. Every order builds a direct line between a household and the farmer who grows for them — your trust goes to the person doing the work, not just the platform in between.
