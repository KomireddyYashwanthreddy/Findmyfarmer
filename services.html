what we run

Four systems, one supply chain.

Each part of Kshetrajiva exists to close the distance between a farm and a kitchen — in trust, in time, and in the number of hands produce passes through.

service 01

Verified farmer profiles

Before anything ships, you can see exactly who grew it. Every farm on Kshetrajiva has a public profile: location, growing practices, what's used on the crop, and ratings from other households who've bought from them.

  • Farm location and grower photo
  • Growing practices and materials used
  • Ratings and reviews from real consumers
service 02

Traceable sourcing

Every order carries a path you can follow — the field it left, the day it was picked, and the route it took to reach you. Nothing about your food's origin stays hidden.

  • Pick date logged at the farm
  • Route recorded from field to home
  • Full history visible on every order
service 03

Neighborhood distribution points

Produce first arrives at a trusted household in your area, not a warehouse across the city. That household acts as a local hub — closer to you, and closer to the community it serves.

  • Local households as distribution nodes
  • Shorter travel time from arrival to pickup
  • A face-to-face handoff, not a locker
service 04

Last-mile delivery

From the distribution point, a local delivery partner brings your order the rest of the way. Prefer to walk over instead? You can collect it directly from the household near you.

  • Local delivery partners for doorstep drop-off
  • Self-pickup option at the distribution point
  • Short, single-neighborhood routes

the result

Fewer hands, fresher produce, fairer prices.

Cutting out the layers between farm and kitchen means produce moves faster, farmers keep more of what you pay, and you always know exactly where your food came from.
