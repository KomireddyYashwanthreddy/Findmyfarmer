the story

Why Find my farmer exists

Most people in Indian cities have no idea who grows their vegetables. By the time produce reaches a kitchen, it has usually passed through three or four intermediaries — each one adding a markup, and each one taking the buyer further from the farm it started at.

Find my farmer was built to close that gap. The name comes from Sanskrit — kshetra, the field, and jiva, the living being — the life of the field, and the people who tend it.

We work directly with farmers around Hyderabad, give every farm a public profile, and route produce through trusted households in your own neighborhood instead of a warehouse across the city. The more a household buys, the stronger its direct relationship with the farmer becomes — trust builds toward the person growing the food, not just the platform in between.
